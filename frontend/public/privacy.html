Twilight Hearth

Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Twilight Hearth (twilight-hearth.net) is a private recipe site run by an individual for invited friends and family.

What we collect

  • From Google when you sign in: your name, email address, whether the email is verified, and your Google account ID. We never receive your password, and we do not keep Google access tokens.
  • What you add: recipes and their earlier versions, photos, comments, ratings and saved recipes. Location and other hidden data are removed from photos when they are uploaded.
  • Recipe imports: when you imported, whether AI was used and what it cost. The file or page you import is not kept, except that a web page's recipe photo is saved with your photos. Only what you choose to save becomes a recipe.
  • Invites: the email address an invite is for, if one was given.
  • Technical logs: IP address, time, page requested and browser type, used to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

Google account information is used only to sign you in, check your invite, and show your name with what you post. Everything else is used only to run the site. We do not sell information, show ads, use analytics or advertising trackers, or use Google account information to train AI.

Who can see it

  • Other members can see your name, the recipes you share with members (including their earlier versions), and your comments. Your ratings appear only as part of an average.
  • Private recipes are shown only to you. The operator can technically access everything stored on the server.
  • Your email address is shown only to you and site administrators.
  • When you import a recipe, we may send the photo, file or page text to Anthropic, whose AI reads it and returns the recipe. Hidden data is removed from photos first, and your name and email are not sent, but anything in the file itself is. Importing from a web address makes our server visit that page.
  • Google handles sign-in, and Cloudflare carries traffic to the site. Encrypted backups may be kept with a storage provider. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies

We use only necessary cookies: one that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and one that lasts up to 15 minutes while you sign in. Cloudflare may set cookies needed for security.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep your information while you are a member. A recipe you delete is hidden from everyone, but a copy stays in our database so that forks can credit it. Copies other members made remain theirs, with your name as credit.

You can ask us to permanently erase your account and content, remove your name from credits, or send you a copy of your information. Erased information may remain in backups for up to 12 months. You can also remove the site from your Google account connections.

Security

Connections are encrypted, access needs an invite, and sign-in is handled by Google. No site can promise perfect security.

Children

The site is not meant for children under 13, and they should not have accounts.

Changes and contact

We will update this page, and tell members about important changes, before using information in a new way. Questions or requests: privacy@twilight-hearth.net.
